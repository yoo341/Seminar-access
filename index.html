Live captions, notes and questions for online seminars and webinars.

Live captions

Captions are made from your microphone, so play the seminar through your speakers or place a second device near it. Headphones will block them. They are automatic and can make mistakes, so do not rely on them for names, numbers or instructions. In Chrome the browser sends the audio to its speech service, so avoid confidential sessions.

Captions will appear here.

Live captions need Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone. In other browsers, use the captions built into your meeting platform and keep using the notes below.
